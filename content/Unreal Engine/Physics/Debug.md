**Console commands**
- `p.Chaos.DebugDraw.Enabled 1`
	- **Must be called so other commands work**
- `p.Chaos.Solver.DebugDrawShapes 1`
	- Red: Not simulated?
	- Blue: Not simulated?
	- Yellow: Simulated (Active)
	- Gray: Simulated (Sleeping)
