
Aside the settings for each platform, you can customize the texts showed on the splash screen.

See `FPlatformSplash::SetSplashText`, the types are listed in `SplashTextType::Type`.

There is also `SetCustomSplashImage` which sets the `GCustomSplashScreenFileName` which is used in `FGenericPlatformSplash::GetSplashPath`.

Related types:
- `FPlatformSplash`
- `FGenericPlatformSplash`
