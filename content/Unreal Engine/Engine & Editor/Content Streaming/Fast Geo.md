# Debugging
- `show ActorColoration FastGeo`: FastGeo content in Blue and Non-FastGeo content in Red
- `FastGeo.Show`: Hide/show FastGeo primitives
- `FastGeo.EnableTransformerDebugMode`: Log transformation details on selection, in PIE or at Cook
